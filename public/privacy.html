Privacy notice

Last updated 18 November 2026. Written to meet the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and its General Application and Implementation Directive (GAID 2025), and the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Who is responsible

Voice Budget is built and run by Joseph Okeoma, Lagos, Nigeria (the data controller). Questions or requests: josephokeoma07@gmail.com.

What we collect, and why

DataWhy
Your name, email address and profile photo from Google, when you sign inTo create your account and show who is signed in
The money entries you make: amounts, pots, notes, dates, savings, income and budget settingsTo run your budget and keep it on all your devices
The words you said or typed for each entrySo you can see what the app heard and fix mistakes

We do not collect your bank login, BVN, card numbers or bank account numbers. We don't keep recordings of your voice.

If you use the app without an account, everything stays in your browser on your phone and nothing is sent to us.

Legal basis

We process your data with your consent (NDPA section 25(1)(a); GDPR Article 6(1)(a)), given when you create an account, and to provide the service you asked for (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). Some of this is financial information, so we treat it with extra care. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

Who handles your data for us

  • Supabase stores your account and entries in a database in London, United Kingdom. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and database rules stop any user from reading another user's entries.
  • Google provides "Continue with Google" sign-in.
  • Your browser's speech service. When you tap the talk button, your browser (for example Chrome, which uses Google's speech service) turns your voice into text. Voice Budget only receives the text.
  • GitHub Pages serves the app's files. It does not receive your entries.

Storing data in the UK is a transfer outside Nigeria. The UK has data protection law comparable to the NDPA, and we rely on your consent and on the processor's contractual safeguards for this transfer (NDPA Part VIII). We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

As long as your account exists. When you delete your account (Me → Delete my account), your profile and every entry are erased from the database straight away. Encrypted backups held by Supabase roll off within their backup window.

Your rights

  • Access and portability: Me → Download a copy gives you all your data as a file.
  • Correction: edit any entry in History, or your details in Me.
  • Erasure: Me → Delete my account.
  • Objection, restriction or withdrawal of consent: email us.
  • Complaints: you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng), or, in the UK/EU, to your local data protection authority.

We reply to requests within 30 days. If a data breach affects you, we'll notify the NDPC within 72 hours and tell you without undue delay.

Children

Voice Budget is for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change this notice in a way that affects you, we'll tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
